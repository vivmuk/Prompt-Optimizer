Be Ambitious Prompting

Felix Rieseberg (Anthropic) says we're not ambitious enough with models. His fix has three levers: Altitude — aim at the goal beyond the goal; Depth — work with the instruments of the field's best, checked by an outside judge; Range — replace the nouns with verbs, then assemble a crew of unexpected fields. Better, not bigger. Based on the article · summarized by RoboNuggets · skill from robonuggets/be-ambitious.

The three types

⛰️ Altitude

Stop fixing single crashes; keep the apps crash-free. Ask: what do you want to happen next? Build a 3-rung ladder: understand → decide → replace the ritual.

🔬 Depth

Use the raw data and real instruments the top experts use, set an "unreasonable" goal and treat it as reasonable, adversarially test what everyone believes — and always install an outside judge.

🎭 Range

A QBR isn't "a deck", it's "getting busy people to believe a story in 30 minutes". Verbs reveal the crew: analyst, journalist, short seller. Do what they do — don't talk like them.
